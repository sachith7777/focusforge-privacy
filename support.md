# FocusForge Support

Welcome to FocusForge Support.

FocusForge helps you build better focus habits through focus sessions, app blocking, challenges, and progress tracking.

## Need help?

If you have questions, experience a problem, or need assistance with FocusForge, contact us:

**Email:** focuforge.app@zohomail.in

Please include a description of the issue and your device's iOS version so we can help you.

## Subscription Support

For subscription billing, renewals, or cancellations, you can manage your subscription through your Apple Account settings.
