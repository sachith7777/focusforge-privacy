# FocusForge Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026  
**App:** FocusForge  
**Developer:** Madhavi Penchala  
**Contact:** focuforge.app@zohomail.in

## 1. Introduction

FocusForge is a productivity and focus application designed to help users build consistent work habits, complete focus sessions, track progress, and reduce distractions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FocusForge processes, how that information is used and stored, and what choices you have regarding your data.

We aim to collect as little information as reasonably necessary to provide the app's features.

## 2. Information We Process

### Information you create in the app

FocusForge may store information you create or generate while using the app, including:

- Focus session durations and completion status.
- Daily activity records, streaks, experience points, and progress statistics.
- Mission and challenge completion records.
- Coach messages and related app preferences.
- App settings and other information required to operate your experience.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device using Apple's data-storage technologies.

### Screen Time and app-selection information

FocusForge may use Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to support distraction-reduction features such as Deep Mode.

Depending on the features you enable and the permissions available to the app, this may involve:

- Your authorization status for Screen Time-related features.
- App or category selections made through Apple's system-provided selection interface.
- Focus-related activity summaries used to display progress.
- Temporary state required to manage distraction restrictions during a focus session.

Apple's system frameworks control access to protected Screen Time information. FocusForge is designed to use the permissions and information made available to it.

Screen Time functionality depends on the required Apple entitlements, permissions, device configuration, and operating-system behavior. Availability may vary. We do not claim that all Screen Time features are available until the necessary Apple approvals and device-level behavior have been verified.

We do not intend to collect the contents of your communications, browsing history, or personal information from other apps through these features.

### Information we do not ask you to provide

FocusForge does not require you to create an account with us to use its core local features. We do not ask you to provide your real name, home address, or phone number to use the app.

Please contact us using the email address above if you have questions about the information processed by the app.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information processed by FocusForge is used to:

- Run and recover focus sessions.
- Display your progress, streaks, missions, and statistics.
- Support distraction-reduction functionality you choose to enable.
- Generate weekly summaries and reports.
- Remember your app preferences and settings.
- Process subscription entitlements through Apple's StoreKit system.
- Export or delete app data when you use the corresponding features.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Data Storage and Network Communications

FocusForge is designed primarily around local data storage on your device.

The app's core focus records, missions, progress information, and related preferences are stored locally. We do not operate a FocusForge account server or cloud-sync service for these records as part of the current version.

FocusForge does not intentionally send your local focus records to our own analytics or advertising servers. We do not use advertising SDKs to build advertising profiles in the current version.

Apple may process information according to its own services and privacy practices when you use iOS, Screen Time, the App Store, or StoreKit. Such processing is governed by Apple's applicable terms and policies.

Your device backups may include FocusForge data according to your device backup settings. We do not operate our own cloud backup service for your app records.

## 5. Subscriptions and Payments

FocusForge may offer paid features or subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

When you make a purchase, Apple processes the payment through your Apple Account. FocusForge does not receive or store your full payment card details.

The app may check purchase information and subscription entitlements through StoreKit to determine whether premium features are available.

Apple handles billing, payment methods, cancellations, and refunds under its applicable policies. You can manage eligible subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

For information about Apple's privacy practices, visit:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 6. Data Retention, Export, and Deletion

FocusForge retains locally stored app records until they are removed by you, deleted through an available app feature, or otherwise removed from your device.

Where available, the app's data-export functionality allows you to export supported app records. Exported files are under your control, and their subsequent storage or sharing is governed by the destination you choose.

Where available, the app's data-deletion functionality removes supported FocusForge records from the app's local storage and performs the associated cleanup implemented by the app.

Deleting FocusForge from your device may remove locally stored app data, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you maintain.

Deleting local app data does not automatically delete purchase records maintained by Apple or data contained in backups you control. Manage purchases and subscriptions through your Apple Account where applicable.

If you have questions about data deletion or export, contact focuforge.app@zohomail.in.

## 7. Permissions and System Features

FocusForge may request access to system features necessary to provide its functionality, including Screen Time-related permissions and notifications where implemented and enabled.

You can manage available permissions through iOS Settings. If you deny or revoke a permission, some features may be unavailable or may work differently.

Apple controls the system permission interfaces and the availability of protected capabilities. The app cannot override Apple's permission decisions.

## 8. Data Security

We aim to protect locally stored information by relying on Apple's platform security and the storage protections available to the app.

No method of electronic storage or software security is completely risk-free. You should keep your device updated, use an appropriate device passcode, and protect any files you export from the app.

## 9. Children's Privacy

FocusForge is not intended to be directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through an account-registration process.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact focuforge.app@zohomail.in so we can review the concern.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of devices
